Support

How can we help?

Easy2See is made by Audrey Tang. Its first App Store release is being prepared and it is not yet available to download.

Contact support

Email au@civic.ai. Please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Please do not attach recordings or other sensitive content. We can usually help without them.

Set up without glasses

Captions and translation work on your phone alone. You do not need glasses or an account.

  1. Open Easy2See and choose "Set up without glasses".
  2. Allow microphone and speech recognition access when asked.
  3. Prepare the language models you want to use (see below).
  4. Choose captions or translation and start.

Set up with glasses

The teleprompter needs supported glasses with a display, such as Even G2. Mentra Live has no display and cannot show captions or teleprompter text. Allow Bluetooth access when asked, then pair the glasses in the app. Some features may also ask for camera, photos or local network access. Each one is optional and only needed for the feature that uses it.

Preparing models

Some language and speech models are downloaded to your phone before you can use them. Connect to the internet when you prepare them. Downloads come from Apple and GitHub hosting, which may see basic connection and request information under their own policies. See the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • An iPhone that can run the app.
  • Microphone and speech recognition access for captions and translation.
  • Supported display glasses, such as Even G2, for the glasses teleprompter.
  • iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence available, for AI cues.

Features

  • Captions and translation: work on the phone without glasses.
  • Teleprompter: shows on supported display glasses, such as Even G2.
  • Recorder: records audio and can export WAV files.
  • Easy2Say: conversation support, with AI cues on supported devices.

Troubleshooting

  • No captions: check that microphone and speech recognition are allowed in iOS Settings, then Easy2See.
  • Glasses not found: check that Bluetooth is allowed and that the glasses are charged and nearby.
  • No AI cues: they need iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence available on your device.

Deleting your data

Easy2See keeps your content on your device. Deleting the app removes its local data. Files you exported or shared elsewhere stay where you saved them, and you need to delete those yourself.

支援

需要什麼協助？

Easy2See 由唐鳳製作。首個 App Store 版本正在準備中，目前還不能下載。

聯絡支援

請寄信到 au@civic.ai。請告訴我們你的 iPhone 機型、iOS 版本，以及問題發生時你正在做什麼。

請不要附上錄音或其他敏感內容。沒有這些，我們通常也能幫上忙。

不使用眼鏡進行設定

即時字幕與翻譯只靠手機就能使用。你不需要眼鏡，也不需要帳號。

  1. 開啟 Easy2See，選擇「不使用眼鏡進行設定」（Set up without glasses）。
  2. 出現提示時，允許麥克風與語音辨識的存取權限。
  3. 準備你想使用的語言模型（見下方）。
  4. 選擇即時字幕或翻譯，然後開始。

使用眼鏡進行設定

提詞器需要支援的顯示眼鏡，例如 Even G2。Mentra Live 沒有顯示器，無法顯示字幕或提詞器文字。出現提示時請允許藍牙存取，然後在應用程式中配對眼鏡。某些功能也可能要求相機、相片或區域網路的存取權限。每一項都是選用的，只有用到對應功能時才需要。

準備模型

部分語言與語音模型需要先下載到你的手機才能使用。準備模型時請連上網際網路。下載來自 Apple 與 GitHub 的代管服務，它們可能依各自的政策看到基本的連線與請求資訊。請參閱隱私權政策。

需求

  • 一支能執行此應用程式的 iPhone。
  • 即時字幕與翻譯需要麥克風與語音辨識的存取權限。
  • 眼鏡提詞器需要支援的顯示眼鏡，例如 Even G2。
  • AI 提示需要 iOS 26 或更新版本，且裝置可使用 Apple Intelligence。

功能

  • 即時字幕與翻譯：不需要眼鏡，在手機上就能使用。
  • 提詞器：顯示在支援的顯示眼鏡上，例如 Even G2。
  • 錄音機（Recorder）：錄製音訊，並可匯出 WAV 檔。
  • Easy2Say：對話輔助，在支援的裝置上提供 AI 提示。

疑難排解

  • 沒有字幕：請到 iOS「設定」，再進入 Easy2See，確認已允許麥克風與語音辨識。
  • 找不到眼鏡：請確認已允許藍牙，且眼鏡已充電並在附近。
  • 沒有 AI 提示：需要 iOS 26 或更新版本，且你的裝置可使用 Apple Intelligence。

刪除你的資料

Easy2See 把你的內容留在你的裝置上。刪除應用程式就會移除它的本機資料。你匯出或分享到其他地方的檔案會留在你儲存的位置，需要由你自行刪除。
