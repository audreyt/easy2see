Words you can see, right when you need them.

Easy2See turns speech into live captions and translation on your phone, and puts a teleprompter on supported display glasses such as Even G2. No account. Your content stays on your device.

Load a script and read it hands-free on the glasses display.

WAV export
Easy2Say

Conversation support that helps you reply, with AI cues on devices that support them.

Built to stay on your device

Captions, translation, teleprompter, Recorder WAV exports and Easy2Say conversation AI run locally. The developer does not receive your speech, transcripts, scripts or recordings.

There is no account to create, and Easy2See does not include app analytics or crash-reporting services. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Built on MentraOS

Easy2See is derived from MentraOS, the open source operating system, app store and development framework for smart glasses, created by Mentra Labs, Inc. and the MentraOS contributors. Easy2See starts from their code and inherits the app, the glasses connection and the miniapp runtime from their work. Thank you to Mentra Labs and to every contributor.

CreditMentraOS is released under the Apache-2.0 license. Source and contributors are on GitHub. Easy2See is an independently modified fork. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mentra Labs, Inc.

Why a fork

MentraOS is a general platform: the Mentra App runs miniapps on your phone and connects to Mentra's cloud services. Audrey Tang's fork is narrower on purpose. It is an account-free, on-device variant that focuses on captions, translation, a teleprompter and local conversation AI.

It disables Mentra cloud sessions and telemetry, so your speech and transcripts stay on your phone. It is built for display glasses such as Even G2, and it also works with only your phone for captions, translation and review.

If you want the full platform, the miniapp store or Mentra's own glasses, use the Mentra App. Mentra Live has no display, so it cannot show captions or teleprompter text in Easy2See. Easy2See is maintained independently; send questions about it to au@civic.ai, not to Mentra.

建立在 MentraOS 之上

Easy2See 衍生自 MentraOS，這是由 Mentra Labs, Inc. 與 MentraOS 貢獻者所創建的智慧眼鏡開源作業系統、應用程式商店與開發框架。Easy2See 以他們的程式碼為起點，從他們的成果繼承了應用程式、眼鏡連線與 miniapp 執行環境。感謝 Mentra Labs 與每一位貢獻者。

致謝MentraOS 以 Apache-2.0 授權條款釋出。原始碼與貢獻者名單都在 GitHub 上。Easy2See 是經過獨立修改的分支版本（fork），與 Mentra Labs, Inc. 沒有隸屬關係，也未獲其背書。

為什麼要做分支版本（fork）

MentraOS 是通用平台：Mentra App 在你的手機上執行 miniapp，並連上 Mentra 的雲端服務。唐鳳的分支版本刻意定位得更窄：它是不需要帳號、在裝置上運作的變體，專注於即時字幕、翻譯、提詞器與本機對話 AI。

它停用了 Mentra 的雲端工作階段與遙測，因此你的語音與逐字稿都留在手機上。它為 Even G2 這類有顯示功能的眼鏡而設計，只用手機也能使用即時字幕、翻譯與回顧。

如果你想要完整的平台、miniapp 商店或 Mentra 自家的眼鏡，請使用 Mentra App。Mentra Live 沒有顯示器，因此無法在 Easy2See 中顯示字幕或提詞器文字。Easy2See 由唐鳳獨立維護；有關它的問題請寄到 au@civic.ai，不要寄給 Mentra。
