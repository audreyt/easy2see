Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The short version: your content is processed and stored on your device. We run no cloud service for it and no app analytics.

Who we are

Easy2See is made by Audrey Tang. Contact: au@civic.ai.

No account

You do not create an account to use Easy2See, and we do not ask for your name or email address in the app.

What stays on your device

Captions, translation, teleprompter scripts, Recorder audio and WAV exports, and Easy2Say conversation AI are processed and stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this content and does not process it in the cloud.

No analytics or crash reporting

Easy2See does not send app analytics or crash reports to the developer. Sentry, Firebase and PostHog are disabled.

Permissions

The app may ask for these permissions. Each is used only for the feature that needs it, and you can change them at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Microphone and speech recognition: to caption, translate and record speech.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to supported smart glasses, including Even G2.
  • Camera and photos: for features that use them, if you choose to use those features.
  • Local network: to talk to your glasses over your local network for features that need it.

When the app uses the internet

Easy2See does not promise to work with no network at all. Some models are downloaded from Apple and GitHub hosting. These providers may process connection and request information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not control or make claims about how long they keep it.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a file, such as a WAV recording, to a destination you choose, that destination's privacy policy and settings apply to the file.

This website

This website is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process standard HTTP request data such as your IP address and browser details to serve pages. The site sets no cookies, has no forms and uses no analytics.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address to reply to you. Please do not send recordings or other sensitive content.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Easy2See removes the content and settings it stores on your device. Files you exported or shared outside the app remain where you put them, so delete those separately if you want them gone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: au@civic.ai.

隱私權

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 1 日

簡短版：你的內容在你的裝置上處理與儲存。我們不為這些內容提供雲端服務，也不做應用程式分析。

我們是誰

Easy2See 由唐鳳製作。聯絡信箱：au@civic.ai。

不需要帳號

使用 Easy2See 不需要建立帳號，我們也不會在應用程式中詢問你的姓名或電子郵件地址。

留在你裝置上的內容

即時字幕、翻譯、提詞器稿件、Recorder 的錄音與 WAV 匯出檔，以及 Easy2Say 的對話 AI，都在你的裝置上處理並儲存於本機。開發者不會收到這些內容，也不會在雲端處理它們。

沒有分析或當機回報

Easy2See 不會向開發者傳送應用程式分析或當機回報。Sentry、Firebase 與 PostHog 皆已停用。

權限

應用程式可能會要求下列權限。每一項只用於需要它的功能，你隨時可以在 iOS「設定」中更改。

  • 麥克風與語音辨識：用於為語音加上字幕、翻譯與錄音。
  • 藍牙：用於連接支援的智慧眼鏡，包括 Even G2。
  • 相機與相片：供使用到它們的功能使用，且僅在你選擇使用那些功能時才會用到。
  • 區域網路：在需要的功能中，透過你的區域網路與眼鏡通訊。

應用程式何時使用網際網路

Easy2See 不承諾能在完全沒有網路的情況下運作。部分模型從 Apple 與 GitHub 的代管服務下載。這些提供者可能依各自的隱私權政策，處理連線與請求資訊，例如你的 IP 位址。我們無法控制他們保存這些資訊多久，也不對此作任何聲明。

匯出與分享

當你把檔案（例如 WAV 錄音）匯出或分享到你選擇的目的地時，該檔案適用該目的地的隱私權政策與設定。

本網站

本網站由 Cloudflare 代管，它可能為了提供網頁而處理標準的 HTTP 請求資料，例如你的 IP 位址與瀏覽器細節。本網站不設定 Cookie，沒有表單，也不使用分析。

電子郵件

如果你寄信給我們，我們會用你的訊息與電子郵件地址來回覆你。請不要寄送錄音或其他敏感內容。

刪除你的資料

解除安裝 Easy2See 會移除它儲存在你裝置上的內容與設定。你在應用程式之外匯出或分享的檔案會留在你放置的位置，如果想讓它們消失，請另外刪除。

變更

如果這份政策有變動，我們會更新此頁面與上方的日期。

聯絡

有關隱私的問題：au@civic.ai。
